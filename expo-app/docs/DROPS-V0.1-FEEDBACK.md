### Performance purpose — correction superseding earlier answers

User: "Your performance score is a number that tells you how good you'll feel based on the data we have exposed to us. For now it is creatine saturation, caffination, hydration, etc. fiber doesnt really play a role."

Performance is intended to estimate how the user will feel using available hydration, caffeine and creatine-status inputs. It is not the target-completion score described earlier in the interview or research/targets-performance.ts. Fiber remains trackable but does not contribute to Performance. The intended feeling, prediction horizon, model assumptions and validation approach require follow-up. Do not preserve the superseded equal-weight four-target formula merely because it is present in the handoff research.

### Performance outcome and horizon — confirmed answers

- Outcome: "physical energy and readiness".
- Horizon: "how you're expected to feel over these 24 hours".

Performance should estimate physical energy/readiness over a 24-hour window. Whether this is rolling from now or today's calendar day, assumptions about future planned intake, and optional subjective check-ins remain unanswered. Do not broaden the outcome to mental focus or all-purpose wellbeing without further instruction.

- Follow-up answer: "11 b" — defer subjective check-ins; use intake data only for v0.1. Empirical validation cannot assume a built-in self-report dataset. Window anchoring and future-intake assumptions remain open.

### Performance window and future intake — confirmed answers

- Window: "rolling from the last 12 hours to the next 12 so it is centered on how you should feel right now."
- Forecast assumption: "forecast as if scheduled doses are taken".

The visible 24-hour timeline is centered on now: now − 12 hours through now + 12 hours. The primary score describes expected physical energy/readiness at now, rather than a day-average or next-24-hours summary. Past/current estimates use actual recorded intake; future estimates assume remaining scheduled doses are taken. Planned doses must remain distinct from actual entries and must never be automatically persisted as consumption. Longer intake history may still be needed internally for caffeine carryover or modeled creatine status despite the 24-hour display window.
